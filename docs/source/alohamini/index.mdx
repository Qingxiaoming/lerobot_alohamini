# AlohaMini

AlohaMini extends LeRobot with support for the AlohaMini mobile dual-arm product line and the
AM-ARM200 arm.

## Choose Your Workflow

- [Installation and configuration](installation.mdx) applies to every supported setup.
- [AlohaMini 1 / 2 / 2 Pro](alohamini.mdx) covers the dual-arm robot with a Raspberry Pi host and PC
  client.
- [AM-ARM200](am-arm200.mdx) covers the single-arm workflow on one PC.

## References

- [Hardware profiles](profiles.mdx) explains `--robot_model` and `--arm_profile`.
- [Command reference](commands.mdx) collects setup, calibration, teleoperation, recording, training,
  evaluation, and debugging commands.
- [Debug tools](https://github.com/Qingxiaoming/lerobot_alohamini/blob/main/docs/modules/examples/debug-tools.md)
  documents low-level motor, wheel, lift, and servo diagnostics.

Operational Genie Sim procedures and dated handoffs are maintained in the repository's
[internal documentation](https://github.com/Qingxiaoming/lerobot_alohamini/blob/main/docs/internal/README.md),
not in the public site.
